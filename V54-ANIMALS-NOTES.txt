AREG Kids Game — V54 Animals Audio

This build continues V53-FIXES(1) and changes only the Animals gallery behavior/audio.

Implemented:
- Rabbit / Polar Bear and other long labels stay above the decorative leaves.
- Animal names are slightly larger/heavier and allow two lines.
- Tap feedback is slower and more polished.
- The CARD itself does not scale over neighboring cards.
- Only the animal image zooms inside its clipped image area, so it cannot cover another card.
- Press -> animal comes forward -> natural Armenian female narration -> animal sound (~3 s) -> smooth return.
- Tapping another animal cancels the previous sequence cleanly.
- Menu music pauses while an animal is speaking and resumes afterward.
- Voice Hints toggle controls the Armenian narration.
- Effects toggle controls animal sounds.
- Master Sound disables both.

Temporary audio:
- 30 Armenian female voice clips and 30 AI animal-vocalization clips are wired through generated temporary URLs.
- These are temporary placeholders exactly for the phase requested: later replace them with the user's own local audio files.
- animal-audio-manifest.json includes the future local filenames for easy replacement.
- There is a browser Armenian speech fallback if a narration URL fails.

Version: V54
