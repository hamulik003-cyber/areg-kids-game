# Phase 2: City of Time

Status: design in progress, not playable or approved.

Six unique chapters, target length 7800 world units:

1. Sky Railway: change three track switches to route a carriage into a one-time bridge.
2. Clock Observatory: set three clock hands to distinct visual clues to unlock the circular passage.
3. Steam Laboratory: route pressure through three valves to power a single elevator.
4. Pendulum Library: repeat a musical sequence to build a staircase of floating books.
5. Sky Gardens: rotate mirrors to direct sunlight to seeds, growing three distinct living bridges.
6. Time Heart: arrange three energy rings in the correct spatial configuration to revive the city.

Every chapter requires a distinct palette, parallax architecture, original interactive animations, puzzle state, meaningful feedback, and nonrepeating obstacles. The previous rejected prototype must never be reused.

Preserve the exact approved Areg character and movement, the complete first stage, and the approved door sequence: stop, open, walk to center, fade, close, then show transition. Implement and QA only on the trial branch, never main without approval. Do not call a design document a finished game.
