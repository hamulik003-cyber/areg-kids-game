Textures are based on NASA elevation and imagery data and are used here as 2K equirectangular maps.


Additional V165/V166 natural-satellite and dwarf-planet textures:
- NASA / USGS / JPL mission mosaics and planetary maps, via open-source solar-system projects.
- chipi/orrery satellite maps (Björn Jónsson planetary maps and Wikimedia/NASA imagery; see upstream texture provenance).
- HowardZlh/stellar-odyssey Ceres map (NASA Dawn global mosaic, public domain).
- Atlas26 satellite maps for selected Uranian/Neptunian moons.
- Solar System Scope fictional dwarf-planet maps for Haumea / Makemake / Eris (CC BY 4.0 where applicable).

Project keeps local copies under assets/space3d/ for stable offline/PWA rendering.
