Textures are based on NASA elevation and imagery data and are used here as 2K equirectangular maps.


V164 first-five seam-safe batch:
- Added Phobos, Deimos, Io, Europa and Ganymede to Space Search without changing the accepted V163 layout/camera/feedback engine.
- Base global mosaics are pinned from Hotragn/HotEARTH commit 8b022bd0f89469ac9395417eb11eca4db876aef0, whose source notes trace them to NASA/JPL/Caltech/USGS mosaics.
- Runtime preprocessing resamples every new body to 2048x1024 equirectangular, fills black polar no-data only, color-grades to the matching Planets-gallery identity, and mathematically blends the ±180° longitude band to prevent a visible seam.
- The five use baked-texture rendering so one hemisphere cannot turn black from scene lighting. Phobos and Deimos keep mild irregular geometry.


V165 reference-projected first-five fix:
- Replaced the first-five external moon mosaics with runtime equirectangular textures derived directly from the existing Planets-gallery reference images 11-phobos.jpg through 15-ganymede.jpg.
- The visible front hemisphere therefore preserves the exact gallery color palette, contrast and landmark identity the child learns first.
- The hidden hemisphere is mirrored from the same reference surface, creating a closed 360-degree texture with identical left/right longitude edge pixels and no visible seam.
- Blue/purple gallery background pixels are rejected during projection so the nebula background cannot leak onto the 3D body.
- V163 camera, three-object layout, animation timing, win/lose feedback and interaction logic remain unchanged.
